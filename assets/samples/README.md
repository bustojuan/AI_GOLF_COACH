# Sample footage

No standalone swing video is bundled. Download sample footage from its source or record your own swings.

To try the application, record **two distinct swings** yourself (or use video you are authorized to process). Film the whole body and club with a fixed camera and good light. Both recordings should show the **same dominant hand and camera perspective** (face-on or down-the-line). Keep each file under 15 seconds and 50 MB; supported formats are MP4, MOV, WebM and AVI.

Two documented external examples are [Taryn Elliott's swing](https://www.pexels.com/video/slow-motion-footage-of-a-man-playing-golf-3780018/) and [Ryan Parkland's swing](https://www.pexels.com/video/golfer-teeing-off-at-scenic-golf-course-38025632/). These are different recordings and were processed together in the release smoke test. Download them directly from Pexels under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license/). Both were treated as down-the-line/right-handed for that test, but framing and player scale differ substantially, so similarity scores from this pair are **not a coaching benchmark**. Neither stock golfer endorses this application.

In the app: upload your swing, process it, select a separate reference, confirm matching hand/view, review or edit the four suggested timestamps, and continue to comparison, scores and local report preview.
